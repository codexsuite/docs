This page follows the series Working Coasts, which holds Salt and Signal and Tide Tables. Mara Quill writes both. A series gives her one Knowledge base and one set of publication settings for the two books, so she records a lighthouse keeper once and uses the entry in either book.

You need at least two books. For every control on the Series screen, see [Series](/ui/series/).

<Shot name="work-series-overview" alt="The Working Coasts series screen with its two books, Salt and Signal and Tide Tables, and the totals for books and words." />

## Start a series from books you already have

A series starts from standalone books. To make one, open the Library and select New series.

1. Type a name in the Name field.
2. Tick the standalone books that belong in it. The line under the list shows the order you picked them in, and the books take the numbers 001, 002 and so on in that order.
3. Select Create series.

<Shot name="work-series-new" alt="The New series dialog with the name Harbour Walks, the standalone books Lamp Room Notes and Chart House Journal ticked, and Create series at the bottom." />

The picked books leave the Standalone group and join the series stack. The shot above uses two extra books so that the Library has standalone books to pick. Working Coasts was made the same way.

To add a book later, or to take one out, use the Series section of that book's settings. See [Series](/ui/series/#add-an-existing-book-or-take-one-out).

## Put the books in order

Book numbers follow the order in Series settings. To change it, open the series from the Library, select Series settings, and move a book with the arrows beside it. Select Save and the numbers change in both books.

<Shot name="work-series-reorder" alt="The Series settings dialog for Working Coasts with the Book order list. Tide Tables has moved above Salt and Signal." />

## Share Knowledge base entries

A series has a Knowledge base of its own. Entries in it show in every book of the series, under the heading `From Working Coasts`. Each book's own entries stay under `This book`.

Edith Marrow is the keeper in Salt and Signal. She also runs the light in Tide Tables, so Mara moves her to the series.

1. Open Salt and Signal and select Edith Marrow in the Knowledge base sidebar.
2. Select the three dots in the entry window, then Move to series.
3. Read the question, `Move Edith Marrow to the series?`, and select Move.

<Shot name="work-series-promote" alt="The Edith Marrow entry in Salt and Signal with the question Move Edith Marrow to the series? and the buttons Cancel and Move." />

The entry now has a Series badge. Mentions and links in Salt and Signal keep working. Open Tide Tables and the entry is in its sidebar, with the same aliases, summary and text.

<Shot name="work-series-shared-entry" alt="Edith Marrow open in Tide Tables, with the Series badge, the aliases Mrs Marrow and E. Marrow, and the sidebar group From Working Coasts." />

An edit to a series entry from either book changes it for both, because there is one entry. If the series already has an entry with the same name, Codex asks before it merges the two. See [Move an entry to a series](/ui/knowledge-base/#move-an-entry-to-a-series).

## Check what the books share

A book in a series sends the series' style guide with every AI draft, followed by its own. The Writing section of Book settings shows the series guide under the book's guide, read-only, when the series has one. See [Book settings](/ui/book-settings/).

Citation style, quote limit and the other publication settings also follow the series. A book that sets its own value keeps it. Book settings marks each value that comes from the series.

## Take a series away

Deleting a series keeps its books. They become standalone, and Codex copies the series entries and labels onto each book first, so Edith Marrow stays in both. See [Delete a series](/ui/series/#delete-a-series).

## Ask an agent to do it

An agent can create a series, rename it and reorder its books with `create_series` and `update_series`. Moving books between series copies Knowledge base entries and an undo cannot reverse that, so the tools refuse until you call again with `confirm` set. See [Writing with an agent](/work/agents/).
