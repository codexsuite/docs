The pages in this section follow one made-up book through Codex. The book is Salt and Signal, non-fiction by Mara Quill about the people who kept the lights and signals of a coast. It is the first book of the series Working Coasts. The second book, Tide Tables, appears only when a page needs a series.

Every name on these pages is invented. You can follow along with your own book, because each step works the same way on any book.

## The seven stages

1. **Series and book.** Mara opens the Library and starts Salt and Signal inside the series Working Coasts. See [Library](/ui/library/) and [Series](/ui/series/).
2. **Structure.** She builds three acts, The Light, The Keepers and The Last Watch, and gives each scene a summary. See [Planning the structure](/work/structure/).
3. **Research.** She adds Edith Marrow, Tobias Venn, Harrowmouth and the Fresnel lens to the Knowledge base, and adds the sources she reads. See [Research beside the draft](/work/research/).
4. **Draft.** She writes each scene, by hand or from a beat. See [Drafting](/work/drafting/).
5. **Clear.** She checks every quote against the publisher's word limit and asks for permission where a quote is over it. See [Clearing quotes and citing](/work/clearing/).
6. **Lay out.** She picks the template `novel-6x9`, previews the pages and fixes what preflight reports. See [Getting to print](/work/print/).
7. **Print.** She exports the PDF. The export needs the worker and a host with Landlock ABI 4. See [Requirements](/start/requirements/).

## Where each stage lives

The book bar has four tabs. Plan is stage 2 and part of stage 3. Write is stage 4. References holds the sources from stage 3 and is where stage 5 happens. Layout is stages 6 and 7. The Knowledge base sidebar sits beside Plan and Write, so research stays in view while you plan and draft. See [Navigating Codex](/ui/navigation/).

## You do not have to go in order

Codex does not make you follow this order. You can add a source while you draft, restructure after you clear a quote, and open Layout before the last chapter exists. Codex saves a few seconds after you stop typing, and when you leave the field.

Two things connect the stages. An entry marked Always in context, or added to a beat, goes into the prompt of an AI draft. A source cited in a scene links References to the text, so archiving the scene changes what References shows. See [Planning the structure](/work/structure/).

An agent can do some of these stages for you over MCP. See [Writing with an agent](/work/agents/).
