## Three acts, six chapters

Salt and Signal has three acts. The Light covers how the lamp at Harrowmouth is built and tended. The Keepers covers the families who lived at the station. The Last Watch covers the machines that take over the lamp and the keepers' last year. Each act holds two chapters, and each chapter holds two scenes, so the book starts with twelve scenes.

A new book has one act. To add the others, select New Act in the toolbar and type the title in the act's header. To add a chapter, select New Chapter in the act's header. To add a scene, select New scene at the bottom of a chapter.

<Shot name="work-structure-new-scene" alt="The first chapter after New scene, with a new empty card at the end and the cursor in its summary." />

## Write a summary for each scene

Type a sentence in each scene's summary. Mara writes what happens and who is there, for example "Edith Marrow lights the lamp for the first time as head keeper." Type `@` in a summary to mention an entry from the [Knowledge base](/ui/knowledge-base/). The summary saves a few seconds after you stop typing.

The summaries make the Outline view useful. Select Outline in the toolbar to see every scene as a row, in book order, with its summary, linked entries, labels and word count. Select Board to go back.

<Shot name="work-structure-outline" alt="The Plan as an outline table with one row for each scene and its summary." />

## Mark what needs work with labels

Mara marks the scenes she has not researched with the label Needs research. Select Label on a scene card to add a label to it. Select Label in the toolbar to show only the scenes that carry one label. Select Manage labels in that menu to rename a label, add one or delete one.

<Shot name="work-structure-labels" alt="The Label filter open, listing Needs research, Ready to write, Drafting and Maybe cut." />

Mara treats Needs research as a reminder to find a source for the scene, which is the next stage. See [Research beside the draft](/work/research/).

## Reorder and restructure

Mara decides that Oil and Brass should come before The Lamp Room. Tab to the grip at the top left of the chapter, then press Alt and the Left arrow. You can also drag the grip. The chapters renumber, so Oil and Brass is now chapter 1.

<Shot name="work-structure-chapter-moved" alt="The Plan after Oil and Brass moved before The Lamp Room, with the keyboard focus still on its grip." />

Moving a chapter keeps its scenes, summaries, labels, linked entries and cited sources. A link to the chapter in your text still points at it and shows its new number. See [Plan](/ui/plan/) for moving scenes between chapters.

## Archive what you cut

The scene Counting the oil may not survive the first edit. Open its menu and choose Archive scene. A toast says what went with it, and Undo brings it back. An archived scene leaves the book, and its cited sources stop counting as cited until you restore it. The Archived button in the toolbar lists everything you archived.

<Shot name="work-structure-scene-archived" alt="The Plan after Counting the oil was archived, with a toast that offers Undo." />

## Merge two scenes

To merge a scene into the one before it, open the card's menu and choose Merge into previous scene. The earlier scene takes the text, summary, labels, entries, beats and sources of the later one.

### The merge is refused

Scene 1.2, The lens arrives, has an AI draft waiting. Codex refuses to merge a scene that has one. The toast says `Could not merge the scene: Apply or discard the draft first.` and nothing changes. Open the scene in [Write](/ui/write/), apply or discard the draft, then merge again. See [AI drafts](/ui/ai-drafts/).
