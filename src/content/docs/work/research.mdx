With the structure in place, Mara Quill collects what Salt and Signal needs to get right. She keeps people, places and ideas in the Knowledge base, and the books and articles she reads in References. The Knowledge base sidebar is open beside Plan and Write. References has its own tab. This page follows her through it. The [Knowledge base](/ui/knowledge-base/) and [References](/ui/references/) pages list every option.

<Shot name="work-research-sidebar" alt="The Plan of Salt and Signal with the Knowledge base sidebar listing Edith Marrow and Tobias Venn under People, Harrowmouth under Places and Fresnel lens under Ideas." />

## Add an entry

The sidebar on the left of Plan and Write lists the entries. Mara has four. Edith Marrow and Tobias Venn are People, Harrowmouth is a Place, and the Fresnel lens is an Idea. To add another, select New Entry, which uses the first category, or the + beside a category's heading. Type the name and press Enter. Then fill in Summary and Content.

<Shot name="work-research-new-entry" alt="The window for the new entry Harbour Board, with its name saved and the summary still empty." />

Codex saves nothing until the entry has a name. If another entry already has the name or an alias, Codex shows the name and the entry that has it, and offers Create anyway. See [Knowledge base](/ui/knowledge-base/).

## Give an entry the names you use in the text

Mara's keeper appears as Edith Marrow, Mrs Marrow and E. Marrow. Under the entry's name, select Alias, type another name and press Enter. Write underlines the name and every alias wherever it appears in your scenes, so you can see which passages are about her.

<Shot name="work-research-underlined" alt="Scene 1.1 in Write with the words Mrs Marrow underlined with a dotted line." />

<Shot name="work-research-alias" alt="The entry Edith Marrow with the aliases Mrs Marrow, E. Marrow and Edie under the name." />

## Link entries to scenes

To link an entry to a scene, drag the entry from the sidebar onto the scene's card on the Plan. The entry appears as a chip on the card. Or select + Codex on the card and pick the entry. To mention an entry in the text, type `@` or drag the entry from the sidebar into the text. In a summary, type `@`. See [Write](/ui/write/).

<Shot name="work-research-linked" alt="The Plan after Harrowmouth was dragged onto the card for The lens arrives, which now shows Harrowmouth as a chip." />

<Shot name="work-research-mention" alt="Scene 1.1 in Write after Edith Marrow was dragged to the end of the underlined words, which adds a mention of the entry." />

An entry that is Always in context goes into the prompt of every AI draft in the book. To add an entry to a beat, select + Context on the beat and pick the entry. It goes into that beat's draft only. See [Drafting](/work/drafting/).

<Shot name="work-research-beat-context" alt="The beat on The lens arrives with Harrowmouth added as a context chip." />

## Record a fact from a scene onward

To record something true of an entry from a scene onward, type `/` in Write and choose Codex addition. Pick the entry and type the note. The note also appears on the entry's Timeline tab under `From scene <number>`. To add a note from the entry instead, open the Timeline tab, select Add a note at..., pick the scene and type the note. To change a note, select Edit on it. If a note comes after the entry's first use, the tab says `This note comes after the entry's first use in scene <number>.`

<Shot name="work-research-addition" alt="Scene 1.1 in Write with a Codex addition card for Edith Marrow holding the note From here Edith Marrow is head keeper and signs the logbook." />

<Shot name="work-research-timeline" alt="The Timeline tab for Edith Marrow with the notes From scene 1.1 and From scene 1.2, and a warning on the second that it comes after the entry's first use." />

## See where an entry is used

Open the entry and select the Used in tab. It lists every scene that touches the entry, grouped by chapter, with the kind of use beside each scene. Edith Marrow is used in four scenes. Select Highlight in Write to open Write with the entry marked in every scene that uses it.

<Shot name="work-research-used-in" alt="The Used in tab for Edith Marrow, listing four scenes with the kinds of use Linked and Name in text." />

## Add the sources

Mara reads two sources. Harbour Press published The Keeper's Ledger, by A. Penhallow, in 1931. Tidewater Review published Signals at Sea, by R. Okafor, in 1978. Both are invented. Open References to see them. The table shows where each source is used, how many words you quote from it, its licence and its clearance.

<Shot name="work-research-sources" alt="The References screen of Salt and Signal with two sources, Signals at Sea marked Copyright risk and The Keeper's Ledger marked Needs check." />

To add a source, select Add source. See [References](/ui/references/) for the fields.

## Cite a source in the text

To cite a source while you write, select the passage in Write, type `/` and choose Source reference. Pick the source from the list. If the source is not there yet, choose New source, add it, and Codex returns you to the text with the citation in place. Codex numbers the citations in book order.

<Shot name="work-research-cite" alt="The source picker opened over a selected sentence in scene 1.1, listing The Keeper's Ledger and Signals at Sea and a New source choice." />

You work through the Clearance column in the next stage. Signals at Sea has a quote over the publisher's word limit, so Codex marks it Copyright risk. See [Clearing quotes and citing](/work/clearing/).
