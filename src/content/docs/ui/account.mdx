Use these pages to sign in, sign out, set your display name, change your password and create the tokens that let an agent work on your books.

**Address:** `/login` to sign in, `/account` for your account, `/account/password` to change a one-time password.

<Shot name="ui-account-overview" alt="The Account page with Profile, Password, Agent access and Signed-in devices cards." />

## Sign in

Codex has no sign-up. An administrator adds your account and gives you a one-time password. The sign-in page says "No account? Ask your Codex admin."

1. Open your Codex address. Without a session, Codex sends you to `/login`.
2. Enter your email and password.
3. Click **Sign in**.

Codex opens the Library. If Codex sent you to the sign-in page from another screen, it takes you back to that screen instead, as long as the address is on your Codex site.

<Shot name="ui-account-sign-in" alt="The Codex sign-in page with an Email field, a Password field and a Sign in button." />

### If the sign-in fails

Codex answers every refused sign-in with the same sentence, whether the email has an account or not:

> Incorrect email or password, or the account is locked for now.

Ten wrong passwords in a row lock the account for 15 minutes. An administrator can unlock it sooner on the [Users page](/ui/users/).

<Shot name="ui-account-wrong-password" alt="The sign-in page after a refused sign-in, with the sentence Incorrect email or password, or the account is locked for now, in red above the Sign in button." />

### Replace a one-time password

When an administrator adds you or resets your password, you sign in with a one-time password. Until you replace it, every screen sends you to `/account/password`. The page says "You signed in with a one-time password. Choose your own password to continue." Fill in the form described under [Change your password](#change-your-password). Enter the one-time password as your **Current password**. Codex then opens the Library.

<Shot name="ui-account-one-time-password" alt="The Change password page with the sentence You signed in with a one-time password. Choose your own password to continue, above the three password fields." />

## The account menu

Your avatar, with your initials, is at the right end of every bar. Click it to open the menu. The menu shows your name and email, then:

- **Account**, which opens `/account`.
- **AI**, which opens your prompt templates and models. See [AI settings](/ui/ai-settings/).
- **Users**, for administrators only. See [User management](/ui/users/).
- **Sign out**, which ends your session and returns you to `/login`.

<Shot name="ui-account-user-menu" alt="The Library with the account menu open under the avatar, listing Account, AI, Users and Sign out." />

## Change your display name

The Account page shows your email, and you cannot change it there. To change your display name:

1. Open **Account** from the menu.
2. In **Profile**, edit **Display name**. It can hold up to 100 characters.
3. Click **Save**. The word "Saved" appears beside the field.

<Shot name="ui-account-name-saved" alt="The Profile card with the display name Alex and Saved beside the Save button." />

Your name appears on books you share and in the user list.

## Change your password

1. In **Password** on the Account page, enter your **Current password**.
2. Enter a **New password** of at least 10 characters.
3. Enter it again under **Repeat new password**.
4. Click **Change password**.

<Shot name="ui-account-password" alt="The Password card on the Account page with the three fields filled in and the Change password button below them." />

Codex shows "Password changed. Your other sessions have been signed out." This session stays signed in. Every other browser and device has to sign in again.

If the two new passwords differ, Codex shows "The new passwords do not match." If the first field is wrong, it shows "The current password is not right." The browser refuses a new password under 10 characters before Codex sees it. The hint under the field reads "At least 10 characters". If the new password is the same as the current one, Codex shows "Choose a new password that differs from the current one."

<Shot name="ui-account-password-mismatch" alt="The Password card with the message The new passwords do not match in red above the Change password button." />

## Agent access

An agent such as Claude Code reaches your books through Codex's MCP server. It signs in with a personal access token. A token acts as you, with only the access you give it. Use this card to create and revoke tokens.

### Create a token

1. In **Agent access**, enter a **Name** that tells you where you will use the token, such as "Claude Code on the desktop".
2. Under **Can**, tick what the agent may do. **Read your books** is always on. The other three choices are:
   - **Edit outlines, prose and the Knowledge base**
   - **Change sources, uses and permission letters**
   - **Ask Codex for drafts (spends your model budget)**
3. Choose **Expires after**: 30 days, 90 days, 1 year or Never. The default is 90 days.
4. Click **Create token**.

<Shot name="ui-account-token-form" alt="The Agent access card with the name Claude Code on the desktop, the write box ticked and 90 days chosen." />

Codex shows the token once, with a **Copy** button. Under it is a ready-made `claude mcp add` command for Claude Code that includes the token. Copy what you need, then click **Done**. Codex stores only a hash of the token, so it cannot show it again. If you lose it, revoke the token and create a new one.

<Shot name="ui-account-token-created" alt="The new token box after Create token, with the token, a Copy button, a Done button and the claude mcp add command." />

### See and revoke tokens

The list under the form has one row per token that is not revoked. Each row shows its name, the start of the token, what it can do, when it was last used and when it expires. A token past its date stays in the list marked "Expired". Click **Revoke** to stop a token at once.

<Shot name="ui-account-token-list" alt="The Agent access card with one token in the list, showing its name, scopes, last used and expiry and a Revoke button." />

The next steps are on [Connect an agent](/agents/connect/). [Tokens and scopes](/agents/tokens/) explains what each choice allows.

## Signed-in devices

The last card on the page lists phones signed in to your account with the Codex mobile app. **Sign out** on a row ends that device's session at once.
