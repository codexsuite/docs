A scene beat is a card inside a scene where you describe what happens next. Codex sends the beat, the text before it and the context entries you pick to your AI provider. It shows the reply as a draft under the beat, and nothing reaches the scene until you apply it. You need an AI provider connected first. See [AI settings](/ui/ai-settings/).

**Address:** `/books/<id>/write`. Beats and drafts live in [Write](/ui/write/). A link ending in `#scene=1.2` opens that scene.

<Shot name="ui-ai-drafts-overview" alt="Scene 1.2 of Salt and Signal in Write, with a scene beat and, under it, an AI draft with the buttons Show what was sent, Apply, Retry and Discard." />

A waiting draft shows in two places besides the scene. On Plan, the scene card has a **Draft pending** tag. In Write, the scene's tick on the minimap is highlighted. Open the scene to review the draft.

## Review a draft that is waiting

A draft panel has the model's name, its word count, and four buttons. It sits under the beat that produced it.

To read what the model was given, select **Show what was sent**. The panel lists the template, the model and the provider, the entries sent as context, the token counts, and the full prompt. Select the button again to close the list.

<Shot name="ui-ai-drafts-sent" alt="The draft panel with Show what was sent open, listing the template, model, context, tokens and the prompt text." />

To put the draft in the scene, select **Apply**. The draft text goes into the scene below the beat, and the book bar shows `Saving…` then `Saved`. A message appears at the bottom with the words applied and an **Undo** link.

<Shot name="ui-ai-drafts-applied" alt="Scene 1.2 after Apply, with the draft text under the beat and a message reading Applied 12 words with Undo." />

Select **Undo** in that message to take the text out of the scene again. The draft comes back and waits for you.

<Shot name="ui-ai-drafts-apply-undone" alt="Scene 1.2 after Undo, with the draft panel back under the beat." />

To throw the draft away, select **Discard**. A message reads `Draft discarded` with an **Undo** link, and Undo brings the draft back.

<Shot name="ui-ai-drafts-discarded" alt="Scene 1.2 after Discard, with no draft panel and a message reading Draft discarded with Undo." />

<Shot name="ui-ai-drafts-discard-undone" alt="Scene 1.2 after Undo on the discard message, with the draft panel back." />

Apply replaces what sits under the beat. If the beat already has text under it, the panel says how much. For example, `Apply replaces the 90 words below. The current text is kept in history.` That text covers every paragraph up to the next beat or the end of the scene. To get it back after you move on, open the scene's version history. See [Write](/ui/write/).

**Retry** asks for a new draft with the same settings. The new draft says `attempt 2`, and the old one goes away once the new one is under way.

## Draft a scene from a beat

This is the full path, in a scene that already has text.

1. Put the caret in the scene where the beat should go, type `/scene`, and choose **Scene beat** from the **AI** group of the `/` menu. A beat card appears.
2. Type what happens in the box on the card, for example `Tobias Venn explains to Edith Marrow why the Fresnel lens turns so slowly.` Codex saves the instruction when you leave the box or generate. You can also give the beat a title.

<Shot name="ui-ai-drafts-new-beat" alt="A new scene beat at the top of scene 1.1 with the instruction typed in and the Generate prose button under it." />

3. Choose a length. **200**, **400** and **600** are the targets. The pencil takes any whole number of words, which you confirm with `Enter`. Choose **+ Context** to add Knowledge base entries the model should know about, such as Tobias Venn. Select an entry's chip to remove it again.

<Shot name="ui-ai-drafts-beat-options" alt="The beat with 400 chosen as its length and a chip for Tobias Venn beside the Context button." />

4. Select the name of the template and model beside **Generate prose**. A list opens with the prompt templates on the left and your provider's models on the right. Choose a template such as **Write in Joel's voice**, then choose a model. The list closes after you pick a model. Codex remembers the choice for this beat until you reload the page.

<Shot name="ui-ai-drafts-picker" alt="The template and model list open on the beat, with the templates Continue writing, Write in Joel's voice and Paraphrase and the model fake-model." />

5. Select **Generate prose**, or press `Ctrl+Enter` in the instruction box. The button stays off until the instruction has text. While the model writes, a panel titled `Generating…` streams the text in. Its **Stop** button ends the draft early and keeps the text received so far, marked `Stopped`. When the model finishes, the panel becomes a draft with the four buttons.

<Shot name="ui-ai-drafts-generated" alt="The beat with Write in Joel's voice chosen and, under it, the AI draft with a note that Apply replaces the 90 words below." />

6. Select **Retry** if you want another try. The meta line now reads `attempt 2`.

<Shot name="ui-ai-drafts-retried" alt="The draft panel after Retry, with attempt 2 in its meta line." />

7. Select **Apply**. The draft text goes in under the beat and replaces what was there.

<Shot name="ui-ai-drafts-beat-applied" alt="Scene 1.1 after Apply, with the 12 draft words under the beat and a message reading Applied 12 words with Undo." />

8. Edit the text by hand. Applied text is plain text in the scene, and Codex saves your edits like any other.

<Shot name="ui-ai-drafts-hand-edit" alt="Scene 1.1 with the sentence Tobias had counted them twice added by hand after the applied text." />

9. To read the scene without the beat cards, choose **Format** in the book bar. In the **Scene beats** row, choose **Hide**. The beats stay in the scene and the text under them stays.

<Shot name="ui-ai-drafts-format-beats" alt="The Format list open with the Scene beats row showing Show and Hide." />

<Shot name="ui-ai-drafts-beats-hidden" alt="Scene 1.1 with the beat cards hidden and only the scene text showing." />

To hide a single beat, select **Hide** on its card. A hidden beat shrinks to one line with a **Show** button. Choose **Show** to bring the card back. The **⋮** menu on a beat moves it up or down, or deletes it either with or without the text under it. Drag the grip on the left to move a beat, or press `Alt` with `Up` or `Down` while the grip has focus. **Clear beat** empties the instruction.

## Fill an empty scene

Use this when a scene has only a summary. The steps use a new scene in chapter 1 of Salt and Signal.

1. On [Plan](/ui/plan/), select **New scene** in chapter 1. Type a summary in the new card. Type `@Fres` and choose Fresnel lens to mention it. Select **Label** on the card and choose a label such as **Needs research**. The summary and the label save when you click away.

<Shot name="ui-ai-drafts-empty-summary" alt="The Plan with a new scene 3 in chapter 1, with a summary that mentions Fresnel lens, the label Needs research, and the text Not written." />

2. On the scene card, select the **⋮** menu, then **Open in Write**. Write opens at the scene, with the same summary and label in the gutter.

<Shot name="ui-ai-drafts-empty-open" alt="Write at the new scene 3, with its summary and label in the gutter and an empty editor." />

3. Type `/scene` in the empty scene and choose **Scene beat**. Type the instruction in the card.

<Shot name="ui-ai-drafts-empty-beat" alt="The new scene with a scene beat and the instruction Tobias Venn shows the new keeper how to read the lens and what to log." />

4. Select **Generate prose**. A draft appears under the beat. Review it, then apply, retry or discard it as above.

<Shot name="ui-ai-drafts-empty-generated" alt="The new scene with a draft under the beat and the buttons Show what was sent, Apply, Retry and Discard." />

## Continue writing

To get a draft from the end of a scene, with no beat, put the caret in the scene, type `/cont` and choose **Continue writing**. Codex writes from where the text stops. The draft panel appears at the end of the scene. **Apply** adds the draft to the end of the scene. Codex uses the template **Continue writing** and your default provider.

<Shot name="ui-ai-drafts-continue" alt="The end of scene 2.1 with an AI draft panel below the last paragraph." />

## If you have no AI provider

Without a provider, a beat shows a link, **Connect an AI provider in Settings**, where the Generate button would be. Select it to open the AI settings. A draft that was already waiting stays under the beat.

<Shot name="ui-ai-drafts-not-configured" alt="A beat with the link Connect an AI provider in Settings where Generate prose would be, and a waiting draft below it." />

If you choose **Continue writing** with no provider, Codex shows a message reading `Connect an AI provider in Settings to draft prose.`

<Shot name="ui-ai-drafts-continue-not-configured" alt="Scene 2.1 after choosing Continue writing with no provider, with the message Connect an AI provider in Settings to draft prose." />

A request that reaches the server without a provider gets `Connect an AI provider in Settings → AI to write drafts.` The app shows the link instead of sending that request, so you see this text only from another client. If the provider rejects your key, the draft panel reads `Draft failed` with the message and **Retry** and **Dismiss** buttons. A provider that says it is rate limiting shows `Rate limited. Try again in 30 s`, and Retry waits for the count. Fix the key in [AI settings](/ui/ai-settings/).

## Who can do this

Drafts need the Edit role or higher. With the View or Comment role, a beat shows its title and instruction, with no grip, no menu, no buttons and no draft panel. See [Sharing a book](/ui/sharing/).

<Shot name="ui-ai-drafts-viewer" alt="Scene 1.2 for a reader with the View role, showing the beat card with its instruction only and no draft panel." />

## Keyboard

| Keys | What happens |
|---|---|
| `Ctrl+Enter` in a beat's instruction | Generates a draft, as **Generate prose** does. |
| `Alt` and `Up` or `Down` on a beat's grip | Moves the beat one place. |
| `Esc` in the template and model list | Closes the list. |

Every other key is in [Keyboard shortcuts](/reference/keyboard/).
