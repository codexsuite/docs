The Knowledge base is where a book keeps what it knows about its people, places and ideas. Each entry has a name, other names it answers to, a summary, longer notes and a timeline of facts that change as the book goes on. Codex uses entries to underline names in your text, to link scenes, and to give an AI draft the background it needs.

**Address:** the Knowledge base has no page of its own. Its sidebar sits on the left of the Plan (`/books/<id>/plan`) and Write (`/books/<id>/write`). Select an entry in the sidebar and its window opens over the screen, and the address gains `#entry=<entry id>`. Open that address to land on the same entry. The Layout screen starts with the sidebar hidden. Use the sidebar button in the book bar to show or hide it.

<Shot name="ui-knowledge-base-overview" alt="The entry window for Edith Marrow open over the Plan of Salt and Signal, with the sidebar on the left listing People, Places and Ideas." />

The sidebar groups entries by category and shows, beside each name, how many scenes use the entry. The window has three tabs: Entry, Used in and Timeline. The panel on the right holds Details, Always in context, Source, Related and the dates.

## Find an entry

Type in Search Knowledge base to narrow the list. The search matches names and aliases, so `toby` finds Tobias Venn through his alias Toby Venn. When nothing matches, the sidebar says `No entries match.`

<Shot name="ui-knowledge-base-search" alt="The sidebar after searching for toby, showing only Tobias Venn under People." />

The funnel beside the search hides categories. Clear a category's checkbox to leave it out of the list, and select Show all to bring every category back. Reload the page and the filter resets.

<Shot name="ui-knowledge-base-filter" alt="The category filter open, with a checkbox for People, Places and Ideas and the Show all button." />

In a book that belongs to a series, entries shared by the series appear under `From <series name>`, and the book's own entries appear under `This book`. See [Series](/ui/series/).

## Add an entry

1. Select New Entry at the top of the sidebar. To add the entry to a particular category, select the plus on that category's heading instead.
2. Type the name and press Enter.
3. Fill in the summary, the notes and the details.

New Entry starts in the first category the book lists.

<Shot name="ui-knowledge-base-new-entry" alt="The New entry in People window with the name Nell Harker typed and nothing saved yet." />

Codex saves nothing until the entry has a name. If you close the window with the name empty, no entry is made.

<Shot name="ui-knowledge-base-new-entry-created" alt="The new entry Nell Harker with its summary filled in, listed under People in the sidebar." />

### The name is taken

If another entry in the book, or in its series, already has the name as its name or an alias, Codex does not create the entry. The window says `"Harrowmouth" is already a name or alias of Harrowmouth.` and shows a Create anyway button. Select Create anyway to make a second entry with the same name, or change the name.

<Shot name="ui-knowledge-base-name-taken" alt="The new entry window with the name Harrowmouth, the message that it is already a name or alias of Harrowmouth, and the Create anyway button." />

The check ignores capital letters, so `harrowmouth` is also taken.

## Write the entry

The Entry tab has a Summary of one or two lines and the Content below it. The summary appears on cards and goes to the AI as short context. The content is free text with a small formatting bar for a heading, a quote, a list and an `@` mention of another entry. Under the content you see a word count and when the entry was last edited.

Codex saves the text a few seconds after you stop typing, when you leave a field and when you close the window. Aliases, the category and Always in context save at once.

The Details part of the panel lists the fields the entry's category has. Select Field to add one of your own, with a label and a value.

## Give an entry other names

Under the name, the line Also matches lists the aliases. Select Alias, type another name and press Enter. Press Escape to drop what you typed. To remove an alias, select the cross beside it.

Write underlines every alias, and the name, wherever it appears in your scenes.

<Shot name="ui-knowledge-base-alias-added" alt="The entry Edith Marrow with a third alias, Edie, added under the name." />

If a name or an alias belongs to another entry, Codex refuses the alias and says which entry has it. For example, adding `The Mouth` to Edith Marrow shows `"The Mouth" is already a name or alias of Harrowmouth.` Select Add anyway to save it on both entries, or change the alias.

<Shot name="ui-knowledge-base-alias-taken" alt="The alias The Mouth typed on Edith Marrow, with the message that it is already a name or alias of Harrowmouth and the Add anyway button." />

Codex saves a short or common alias but warns you. An alias of three letters or fewer, or one that matches 25 or more words in the book, gets a message such as `Matches 31 other words in the book. Underlines every "the".`

## Rename an entry

To rename an entry, change its name at the top of the window and press Enter or click away. A box appears with `Keep "<old name>" as an alias`. It is ticked, so text that used the old name still counts as this entry. Clear the box to drop the old name.

<Shot name="ui-knowledge-base-rename" alt="The name Fresnel lens changed to Fresnel optic, with the box Keep Fresnel lens as an alias ticked." />

The reference id on the right of the tabs, such as `e:fresnel-lens`, stays the same when you rename the entry. Select the copy button beside it to copy the id.

If you clear the name, the entry keeps its old name and the window says `An entry needs a name.` If another entry has the new name, the window shows the reason and a Rename anyway button.

## See where an entry is used

The Used in tab lists every scene that touches the entry, grouped by chapter in book order. The count at the top gives the number of scenes and how many fall under each kind: Mention, Summary, Linked, Beat, Beat context, Timeline, Printed and Name in text.

<Shot name="ui-knowledge-base-used-in" alt="The Used in tab for Edith Marrow, listing the scenes that use her, with the kind of use beside each scene." />

Each row has an Open in Write link. Two buttons change how a scene uses the entry.

- Make mention turns the first plain use of the name in the scene into a mention. It shows for scenes whose kind is Name in text.
- Unlink removes a link between the scene and the entry. It shows for scenes whose kind is Linked. A toast offers Undo.

Highlight in Write opens Write with the entry highlighted in every scene that uses it. The scenes you linked on the Plan by dragging an entry onto a scene card show as Linked here.

If no scene uses the entry, the tab says `No scene uses this entry yet.`

## Keep a timeline

The Timeline tab holds facts that are true from a point in the book onward. It starts with `Start of book`, and each note follows in book order under `From scene <number>`.

To add a note, select Add a note at…, choose the scene, type the note and select Add note. Codex puts the note at the start of that scene as a Codex addition, so you can also see and edit it in [Write](/ui/write/). Select Edit on a note to change its text, and Save to keep the change. A toast offers Undo after an edit.

<Shot name="ui-knowledge-base-timeline-add" alt="The Timeline tab for Edith Marrow with the add form open: the scene list and the note text From here Edith is head keeper and signs the logbook." />

<Shot name="ui-knowledge-base-timeline" alt="The Timeline tab after the note was added, listed under From scene 1.1." />

A note must have text. An empty note is refused with `Write the note first.` A note placed after the entry's first use in the book shows `This note comes after the entry's first use in scene <number>.` The warning only informs you, and nothing is blocked.

## Relate entries and link a source

Related, in the panel, lists the entries linked to this one. Select Link entry to pick another entry, and select the cross on a chip to unlink it. The link shows on both entries.

<Shot name="ui-knowledge-base-related" alt="The Link entry picker open on Edith Marrow, listing the other entries in Salt and Signal." />

Source shows the source from [References](/ui/references/) that the entry points to, with its status. Select Open in References to see it. An entry with no source says `No source linked.`

## Always in context

Tick Always in context and Codex sends the entry's name and summary with every AI draft in the book, even when the beat does not mention it. Entries you add to a beat go with that draft whether or not this box is ticked. See [Beats and AI drafts](/ui/ai-drafts/).

## Change an entry's category

Select the category name at the top left of the window to open the list. Pick a category to move the entry there. Select New category to make one, give it a name and pick one of five colours, then select Create. Codex refuses a name the Knowledge base already has with `The Knowledge base already has a category named Laws.`

To rename or recolour a category, or to delete it, point at its heading in the sidebar and select the three dots. Type a new name and select Rename, or pick a colour. Delete category works only on an empty category. A category with entries says `Move or delete its 3 entries first.` The category list for a series appears under `From <series name>` and has no menu here.

<Shot name="ui-knowledge-base-category-menu" alt="The category menu open on the People heading, with the name box, the Rename button, five colours and Delete category." />

## Merge two entries

If you have two entries for the same thing, open the one you want to remove, select the three dots and choose Merge into. Pick the entry to keep. The dialog says what moves, for example `2 scene links, 0 additions, 0 related. "Harrow Mouth" becomes an alias.` Select Merge to confirm.

<Shot name="ui-knowledge-base-merge" alt="The Merge Harrow Mouth into dialog with Harrowmouth chosen and the count of what moves." />

Scenes and mentions that pointed to the merged entry now point to the one you kept, and the old name becomes an alias of it. The window closes and a toast says `Merged <name> into <target>`. The merged entry is archived.

## Delete an entry

To delete an entry, open its three dots menu and choose Delete entry. The dialog says how many scenes use it. For an entry in use it reads `It is used in 4 scenes. Mentions turn into plain text, links and beat context are removed.` Timeline notes are deleted as well. Select Delete to confirm.

<Shot name="ui-knowledge-base-delete" alt="The Delete Edith Marrow dialog, saying the entry is used in four scenes and what happens to them." />

A toast says `Deleted <name>` with an Undo button. Undo brings back the entry, its mentions and its links. If a scene or its text changed in the meantime, Undo refuses with `Could not undo: a scene or text changed after the entry was deleted, so it stays deleted.` and the entry stays deleted.

## Move an entry to a series

In a book that belongs to a series, the three dots menu has Move to series. It makes the entry visible to every book in the series, and mentions and links keep working. If the series already has an entry with that name, the dialog offers Merge into the series entry or Cancel. A series entry shows a Series label beside its category. Only someone with write access to the series can change it.

## Books shared with you

With the View or Comment role the sidebar and entry windows read only. New Entry, the category menus and the three dots menu do not appear, and you cannot drag entries onto scenes. With the Edit role you can change everything on this page. See [Sharing a book](/ui/sharing/).

## Keyboard

| Keys | What happens |
|---|---|
| Escape | Closes the entry window. |
| Enter in the name box | Saves the new name. |
| Enter in the alias box | Adds the alias. |
| Escape in the alias box | Drops what you typed. |
