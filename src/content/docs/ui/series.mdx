The Series screen shows the books in one series, such as Salt and Signal and Tide Tables in the Working Coasts series, with their totals. Use it to set the book order and to archive or delete the series. A book in a series shares the series' Knowledge base, labels, style guide and publication settings. The screen is at `/#series=<id>`. To open it, select a series stack in the [Library](/ui/library/). Select a book to open it on the screen you last used in that book, or on its Plan if you have not opened it before. In the book, the back arrow in the top bar returns to the series.

<Shot name="ui-series-overview" alt="The Working Coasts series screen with its totals and its two books." />

The breadcrumb reads Library, then the series name. Select Library to go back. The header shows four totals for books that are not archived.

- Books is the number of books.
- Words is the word count of all books.
- Published is the number of published books.
- Last edit is when any book last changed.

Below the header are the books in order. The same Grid and List switch as the Library applies. Select `+ Add book` to open the New book dialog with this series already chosen and the next number filled in. This creates a new book. To bring in a book you already have, see [Add an existing book or take one out](#add-an-existing-book-or-take-one-out).

## Change the name, description or book order

1. Select Series settings.
2. Edit the Name or the Description.
3. Under Book order, drag a book by its grip, or focus the grip and press Alt+Up or Alt+Down. The arrow buttons on each row do the same. Book numbers follow this order.
4. Select Save.

<Shot name="ui-series-settings" alt="The Series settings dialog with the name, description and the book order list." />

<Shot name="ui-series-reordered" alt="The Working Coasts series screen with Tide Tables as Book 001 after you move it up and save." />

A series needs a name. If you clear it, the dialog says `A series needs a name.` and saves nothing.

A note in the dialog says that every book in the series shares the series' Knowledge base categories, such as Characters and Places, and that each book can add its own. To move a Knowledge base entry up to the series, see [Move an entry to a series](/ui/knowledge-base/#move-an-entry-to-a-series). A series entry is one entry, so a change you make from any book shows in every book in the series.

<Shot name="ui-series-shared-entry" alt="The Salt and Signal Plan with Harrowmouth moved to the Working Coasts series and its summary changed from Tide Tables." />

## Add an existing book or take one out

Both start in the book, not on the Series screen. Open the book, select Settings, then the Series section. See [Book settings](/ui/book-settings/).

1. Choose the series in the Series list, or choose None (standalone) to take the book out.
2. Select `Review move…`. The dialog lists each step. When a book joins, same-named categories and labels merge. When it leaves, Codex copies the series' Knowledge base entries and labels onto it. The dialog warns that moving the book back does not merge the copies again.
3. Select Move book.

A book you add joins as the next number. When a book leaves, Codex renumbers the books after it. Only the owner can move a book.

## Archive a series

Select Series settings, then Archive series. The series disappears from the Library at once, with no confirmation. A message says `Working Coasts archived. Restore it from the Archived list.` Its books show as archived while the series is archived. Archiving also ends sharing on the series, and Restore series does not bring it back. People who saw the books through the series stay locked out until you share the series again. To restore, choose Archived in the Library's Status control and select Restore series.

<Shot name="ui-series-archived" alt="The Library's Archived list with the Working Coasts series and its Restore series button." />

## Delete a series

Deleting a series keeps its books. They become standalone.

1. Select Series settings, then `Delete series…`.
2. Read the dialog. It states how many books become standalone, and which Knowledge base categories, entries and labels of the series Codex copies onto each book first.
3. Select Delete series.

<Shot name="ui-series-delete" alt="The delete series dialog stating that two books become standalone." />

A message says `Working Coasts deleted. Its books are standalone.` The deleted series appears in the Library's Archived list as `Series · no books`, with Restore series. Restoring it brings back an empty series, without its books. Deleting a series also ends sharing on the series. People who saw its books through the series lose access, unless the book itself is shared with them.

<Shot name="ui-series-deleted" alt="The Library's Archived list with the deleted Working Coasts series, reading Series, no books, and its Restore series button." />

If the delete fails, the dialog shows the server's message. Only the owner can delete a series.

## Series shared with you

A series that someone shared with you shows who shared it and your role. It has no Series settings and no `+ Add book`. If the owner shared only some of the books, a note says only those books appear.
