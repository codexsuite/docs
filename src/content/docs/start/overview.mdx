Codex is a self-hosted writing studio for non-fiction books and series. Novels work too. You plan the structure, keep your research beside the draft, check every quote against copyright limits and export a print-ready PDF.

:::note[Not that Codex]
Codex at codexsuite.app is an independent, open-source writing studio for authors. It is not affiliated with OpenAI or its Codex coding agent.
:::

## The six workspaces

Every workspace reads the same chapters, sources and entries, so you never copy anything between them.

| Workspace | What it does |
|---|---|
| [Library](/ui/library/) | Shows every book and series on one shelf. Progress shows against each book's word target. |
| [Plan](/ui/plan/) | Shows acts, chapters and scenes as a board or a plain outline. Each scene has a summary, a word count and labels such as *Needs research*, and you can pin knowledge base entries to it. |
| [Write](/ui/write/) | Holds the manuscript editor with the plan in the margin. You can draft a scene from its beats with AI, type `@` to link a knowledge base entry, and use focus mode and a minimap. |
| [Knowledge base](/ui/knowledge-base/) | Holds one entry per person, idea or place. Write underlines each use of an entry's name or alias, and Ctrl or Cmd+click opens the entry. Each entry lists the scenes that use it and has a timeline of notes that hold from a point in the book onward. |
| [References](/ui/references/) | Lists every source you quote, where you used it and whether you may print it. Codex counts quoted words against the publisher's limit, drafts permission letters and builds the bibliography in your citation style. |
| [Layout](/ui/layout/) | Shows a live preview of the book in its print template and makes a proof when you need exact pages. Preflight flags low-resolution images, and the export dialog offers bleed and crop marks. |

## How a book is organised

A series holds books. A book holds acts, an act holds chapters, and a chapter holds scenes. A scene is the smallest piece on the Plan board. A series is optional, and a book can stand alone.

Sources and knowledge base entries attach to scenes. That link is how Codex shows where you used a source, and which scenes mention a person.

A series shares its style guide and settings with its books. A book uses the series' setting unless it sets its own, and AI drafts get both style guides. [Series](/ui/series/) explains how.

## Saving and themes

Codex saves as you type, so there is no Save button. It has a light and a dark theme, and every screen follows the one you pick.
